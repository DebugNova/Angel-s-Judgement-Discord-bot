# ANGEL'S JUDGEMENT
### The official 1v1 ranking system of Seven Angels

Every member of the clan now carries a rating, and every duel counts. Challenge anyone in the clan, settle it in Gakuran, and the result is recorded for all to see. Win and you ascend. Lose and you fall.

Read this once and you are ready to fight.

> **Seven Angels. One Judgement.**

## The short version
**1.** Challenge a member with `/1v1`.
**2.** They accept, and a private match room opens for the two of you.
**3.** Play the duel in a private server.
**4.** One player reports the winner, the other confirms it.
**5.** Ratings, stats and the leaderboard update instantly.

That is the entire loop. Everything below is detail.

## How to play a ranked duel

**Step 1: Challenge**
Type `/1v1` and pick your opponent. They have **60 seconds** to press **Accept** or **Decline**. You can withdraw with **Cancel Challenge** before they answer.

**Step 2: Enter the room**
Once accepted, the bot opens a private channel that only the two of you and the staff can see. Every match gets its own ID, for example `SA-000012`.

**Step 3: Share the server**
One of you presses **Submit Server Link** and pastes a Roblox private server link. The link stays inside your room and is never posted anywhere else.

**Step 4: Fight**
Play the duel. If anything goes wrong, drop screenshots or clips in the room. The bot saves them as evidence automatically.

**Step 5: Report**
When it is over, either player presses **Report Result** and selects the winner.

**Step 6: Confirm**
The other player presses **Confirm Result**. The match is final, ratings update, and the result is posted in #match-history.

The room closes shortly after and is deleted 10 minutes later.

## Rating and ranks

Every player starts at **1000 ELO**.

Beating a higher rated opponent earns you more points. Beating a lower rated one earns fewer. Losses work the same way in reverse. The system rewards facing strong opponents, not farming weak ones.

A win against an equal opponent is worth **24 points**. Upsets are worth up to **48**.

Your rating places you in one of the **Seven Choirs**:

**Seraphim** · 1600 and above
**Cherubim** · 1400 to 1599
**Thrones** · 1250 to 1399
**Dominions** · 1100 to 1249
**Virtues** · 1000 to 1099
**Powers** · 900 to 999
**The Fallen** · below 900

Everyone begins among the Virtues. Where you go from there is up to you.

## The leaderboard
You appear on the leaderboard after **5 completed matches**. This keeps a lucky 1 and 0 start from topping the board. The live top ranks are always shown in #leaderboard.

## Disputes and cancellations

**If you disagree with a result**
Press **Dispute Result** instead of confirming. The match is frozen and a referee is called to your room. They will review the evidence and may ask both players for screenshots or clips. Their decision is final.

A report on its own never changes anyone's rating. Nothing is official until both players agree or a referee rules.

**If you need to stop a match**
Press **Request Cancel**. Your opponent chooses **Agree to Cancel** or **Keep Playing**. A match can only be cancelled if both players agree, or by staff. A cancelled match does not count and no rating changes.

**Fair play**
Record your duels when you can. Evidence settles disputes quickly.
Do not report false results or refuse to confirm a fair loss. Staff can see every match and can overturn results or restrict players who abuse the system.
Staff can never judge a match they are playing in.

## Commands

**Playing**
`/1v1 @player` · challenge a member (same as `/challenge`)
`/currentmatch` · jump back to your match in progress
`/match cancel` · ask your opponent to cancel the current match

**Your record**
`/stats` · your rating, choir, rank, record, win rate and streaks
`/stats @player` · someone else's profile
`/history` · your past matches and rating changes
`/headtohead @player` · your record against one player

**The clan**
`/leaderboard` · rankings by ELO, wins, win rate, streak or matches played
`/help` · a full guide inside Discord
`/about` · the story of Angel's Judgement

## Good to know
You can only be in one match at a time.
After a duel you must wait a moment before challenging the same player again.
A player can only hold a few pending challenges at once, so do not spam.
If the bot goes offline mid match, nothing is lost. Your match picks up where it left off.

Questions go to the staff. Everything else is settled in the arena.

> **Every duel is a trial. Every trial ends in judgement.**
